AI From Scratch

Big ideas. Zero coding degree. Let's see what happens.

I'm an entrepreneur, a mom, a grandma, and a 46-year-old woman who has decided that artificial intelligence is entirely too fascinating to leave to the tech experts.

Do I know how to code? Not yet.

Do I have ideas? More than I know what to do with.

I'm learning AI from the ground up, exploring platforms like Hugging Face, experimenting with tools, and figuring out what everyday people can actually accomplish with this technology.

What I'm Doing Here

- Learning AI and coding from scratch.
- Exploring Hugging Face and its open-source AI community.
- Testing AI tools from a real beginner's perspective.
- Documenting what works, what doesn't, and what makes absolutely no sense.
- Turning ideas into real projects.

My Mission

I believe there's a huge audience of people who are curious about AI but have no idea where to begin.

I want to help bridge that gap.

Not by pretending to be an expert, but by becoming one step more knowledgeable every day and bringing other beginners along for the ride.

The Experiment

Can a woman with no computer science degree learn to build useful things with AI?

We're about to find out.

Follow along. This should be interesting.
